About M's Manga

Your trusted source for manga since 2020 based in the UK!

Our Story

M's Manga was founded by myself, Mihai in 2020, before the covid-19 pandemic shut everyone in doors. Manga has always been a huge part of my life and it is what kindled my enthusiasm towards reading and storytelling.

I saw how many struggled during the pandemic with mental-health and thought that making manga more accessible for others would've brought positive change in their lives, allowing them to escape from the dread that was the pandemic.

My Mission

Aim to make manga accessible to everyone. Whether you're a long time reader or new to the world of manga, I want there to be a book for everyone to enjoy. Renowned classics, hidden gems, my goal is to have them all for the masses to access.
